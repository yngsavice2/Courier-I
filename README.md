# Courier

Courier is an original browser delivery-driving game inspired by the satisfying loop of long-haul trucking simulators: accept a contract, plan a route, drive safely, manage fuel, and upgrade your vehicle. It uses original code and canvas visuals and does not include Euro Truck Simulator 2 assets or trademarks.

## Play
Open `index.html`. Use WASD or arrow keys to drive, Space to brake, E to deliver at the destination, M for the map, and P to pause.

## Design analysis
Courier distills the strongest simulator ideas into a short-session game: meaningful jobs, readable hubs and roads, light fuel/damage/time management, ambient scenery, and persistent progression through earnings and upgrades.
